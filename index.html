One sentence. One color. One day at a time.

One Line is a quiet daily journal for people who do not want to write a page. Your journal stays encrypted on your active phone, with optional replacement-phone recovery through your own hidden Google Drive folder.

Your journal belongs to you

One Line does not use your writing for ads or model training. One Line and Supabase do not receive your hidden Google Drive recovery package.

One active journal phone

Connect Google Drive from Settings when you want replacement-phone recovery. The same Google account can restore the journal without a separate code. Journals are not synced or merged across phones.

Simple by design

Write one short sentence, choose one color, and revisit your days by day, month, or year.
