One Line Journaling — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

One Line Journaling is a private journal. This page says, in plain words, what we keep and what we will never do with it.

Account data One Line stores

Supabase stores your email address, first name, account settings, random account and session IDs, one-phone security records, and deletion records. We use them to sign you in, restore your settings, keep one active phone, protect the service, and delete your account.

One Line does not send sentences, drafts, searches, or journal colors to Supabase. The account database and its recovery copies contain account data, not journal entries. We cannot certify erasure of provider-held trash, file versions, or disaster-recovery copies.

Your journal stays under your control

The authoritative journal lives in an encrypted SQLCipher database on your active phone.

Connect Google Drive from Settings to keep a recovery package in the chosen Google account's hidden app data folder. If you lose this phone before its latest writing reaches Google Drive, you may lose that writing. Without Google Drive recovery, you may lose the whole journal.

That package contains an encrypted journal archive and the high-entropy key needed to open it. One Line’s servers and Supabase do not receive either value. Access to the chosen Google account is enough to restore the journal, so someone who gains that Google account and its hidden app data could also recover it. The original One Line sign-in account is not required for recovery into an empty journal.

Google Drive access

The app asks Google only for permission to use its own hidden app data folder. It cannot read any other file in your Drive. The app reads the chosen Google account's email address and stable Google user ID on your phone only to keep the journal linked to the same Drive account; it does not send them to Supabase.

The recovery package is hidden from the normal Drive file list and from other Drive apps. Deleting your One Line account leaves it in your Google account. You control it and can delete it from Google Drive settings under Manage apps.

Usage data

One Line does not currently send product analytics, button counts, search history, crash reports, or performance telemetry. If that changes, we will update this policy and the store disclosure before collection begins.

We don't sell your data, show ads, use advertising trackers, or train a model on your journal.

Technical connection details

Supabase receives basic connection details when One Line signs you in or uses account services, such as your IP address, country, and the Supabase software library version.

Supabase uses its connection and sign-in logs to protect accounts and enforce rate limits. These are required service records, not advertising. One Line does not intentionally put your journal words into connection or sign-in logs.

Email, no spam ever

The only emails we send are your 6-digit sign-in code and replies when you contact us, including a confirmation exchange before we act on an emailed account request. If you email us about your account, we keep that correspondence and our notes about the request for up to 90 days after it is resolved, then delete them. No newsletters, no marketing. If something important changes, we will tell you inside the app.

Reminders & sharing

The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone itself, not sent from our servers.

Share images are created on your phone and handed to the iOS or Android share sheet. We never see or store what you share.

Deleting your account & data

Settings → Delete account & data starts permanent deletion. When the server confirms it, One Line erases your account, profile, settings, sessions, and security records from our active database. The encrypted journal database, its protected local key, and the Drive connection are then removed from the active phone.

Deleting your One Line account does not delete the recovery package in your Google Drive. That package belongs to your Google account and is kept so you can control it independently. To erase it, open Google Drive settings, choose Manage apps, find One Line Journaling, and use Options → Delete hidden app data.

If the phone loses the server’s final answer, One Line locks the journal and keeps a small encrypted cleanup record until it can confirm whether deletion finished. The record may contain the account’s random ID and sign-in email, and is removed when cleanup is proven.

The app supports one active journal phone. Approving a sign-in on a replacement phone removes the previous phone's server authority before the replacement restores its journal. A previous phone that stays offline cannot be remotely stopped or erased. When it next connects, it cannot update the Drive backup; its encrypted local copy remains until the app is removed or account-deletion cleanup finishes on that phone.

If you already deleted the app, email onelinejournaling@gmail.com from the account email. We will erase the One Line account and server data within 30 days. We cannot delete the recovery package inside your Google Drive account.

Current database recovery copies contain account and sign-in records, not journal entries. Copies in provider-held trash, file-version histories, or disaster-recovery systems may persist outside our control. Our normal cleanup keeps the newest account-only recovery copy and may keep an earlier copy for two weeks or longer when delivery cannot be confirmed. Automatic pruning is currently paused during this review, so we cannot promise a two-week deletion date. If new copies stop, the last verified recovery copy may be kept longer until a replacement is safely stored.

After account deletion, one word-free marker may remain for 60 days. It contains the random account ID, deletion time, and a scrambled cleanup verifier so a previously active installation can learn that the account was deleted. It contains no email, name, recovery code, or journal content.

Current provider details: Supabase processes account hosting, authentication, and security logs in the United States. One Line keeps encrypted account-database recovery copies on the operator’s Mac and in the operator’s iCloud Drive and Google Drive accounts. Those copies contain account and sign-in records, including sign-in emails and any stored password hashes, but no journal entries. Brevo processes sign-in email and keeps delivery logs for one month. Google Drive stores the recovery package under the Google account you choose. For account-support cases we use Gmail for the thread, a private Google Drive case file, Google Calendar for reminders, and a private case index on the operator’s Mac. The local index stores case references, topics, statuses and dates without copying journal writing or email bodies. On or before the case’s delete-by date, no later than 90 days after resolution, we permanently delete each thread, case file, reminder and local case record from our accounts and Mac. These providers may still retain limited security, legal, disaster-recovery, or anonymized information under their own policies.

Children

One Line Journaling is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
