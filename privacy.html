One Line Journaling — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

One Line Journaling is a private journal. This page says, in plain words, what we keep and what we will never do with it.

Account data One Line stores

Supabase stores your email address, name, account settings, random account and session IDs, one-phone security records, and deletion records. We use them to sign you in, restore your settings, keep one active phone, protect the service, and delete your account.

One Line does not send sentences, drafts, searches or per-entry journal records to Supabase. Optional usage may include combined colour counts, without individual entries or account IDs. The account database and its recovery copies contain account data, not journal entries. We cannot certify erasure of provider-held trash, file versions, or disaster-recovery copies.

Your journal stays under your control

Your writing is saved on this phone in an encrypted database. The app encrypts it automatically using a key kept in the phone’s protected storage; you do not choose an encryption password.

Turn on Google Drive backup in Settings to save a recovery file in the Google account you choose. It is hidden from the normal Drive file list. If you lose the phone before new writing is backed up, that writing may be lost. With backup off, losing the phone may mean losing the whole journal.

The recovery file contains your encrypted journal and the key that opens it. The app sends this file directly to your chosen Google account, without uploading it to One Line’s servers or Supabase. To recover on a new phone, sign in to One Line and connect that same Google account. Anyone who can connect that Google account to One Line can recover its backup into an empty journal, even with a different One Line sign-in email. No separate recovery code is required.

Google Drive access

The app asks Google only for permission to use its own hidden app data folder. It cannot read any other file in your Drive. The app reads the chosen Google account's email address and stable Google user ID on your phone only to keep the journal linked to the same Drive account; it does not send them to Supabase.

The recovery package is hidden from the normal Drive file list and from other Drive apps. Switching off Google Drive backup in Settings stops backup, deletes this journal’s recovery package from that Google account, and removes One Line’s Google authorization. This may disconnect One Line’s Drive access on other phones using that Google account; your writing stays on the phone and your One Line email login stays signed in. Deletion or access removal that is not confirmed stays unfinished and can be retried in Settings. You can also delete the recovery package from Google Drive settings under Manage apps.

Usage data

With Help improve One Line on (Share usage totals in older versions), current versions send daily totals for app openings, saved lines, backup status, and use of screens, buttons, colours and Share. Newer versions count colours on successful saves and edits; older versions count colour-selector changes. Totals include Android or iOS, but not your writing, search terms, shared images, account details or the time of each action. The totals are stored without your account ID. Sharing is on by default; turn it off in Settings to stop new reports and delete unsent totals. Totals already combined cannot be removed for one person. Share counts mean opening the share options, not sending an image. Normal connection and sign-in security logs are separate. We do not send crash reports or performance telemetry. Older app versions may report fewer totals.

We don't sell your data, show ads, use advertising trackers, or train a model on your journal.

Account problems

If account deletion fails or its result cannot be confirmed, One Line may send your verified account email and a short reason to support so we can investigate. Reports never contain journal writing and are separate from optional usage sharing. Reports in the private queue are normally removed within 30 days, including after account deletion; support emails are kept for up to 90 days after the case is resolved. Offline reports may arrive later or not reach us.

Technical connection details

Supabase receives basic connection details when One Line signs you in or uses account services, such as your IP address, country, and the Supabase software library version.

Supabase keeps connection and sign-in logs to protect accounts and limit repeated requests. The app does not send your journal entries with connection or sign-in requests.

Email, no spam ever

The only emails we send are your 6-digit sign-in code and replies when you contact us, including a confirmation exchange before we act on an emailed account request. If you email us about your account, we keep that correspondence and our notes about the request for up to 90 days after it is resolved, then delete them. No newsletters, no marketing. If something important changes, we will tell you inside the app.

Reminders & sharing

The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone itself, not sent from our servers.

Share images are created on your phone and handed to the iOS or Android share sheet. We never see or store what you share.

Deleting your account & data

Settings → Delete account & data starts permanent deletion. When the server confirms it, One Line erases your account, profile, settings, sessions, and security records from our active database. The encrypted journal database, its protected local key, and the Drive connection are then removed from the active phone.

Deleting your One Line account from Settings first deletes this journal’s recovery package from the Google account connected on that phone. If copy or Google access removal cannot be confirmed, the account is not deleted. Reconnect to Google Drive and try again, or contact support. A package the phone cannot reach, such as one in a Google account it is not connected to, stays there. To erase it, open Google Drive settings, choose Manage apps, find One Line Journaling, and use Options → Delete hidden app data.

If the phone loses the server’s final answer, One Line locks the journal and keeps a small encrypted cleanup record until it can confirm whether deletion finished. The record may contain the account’s random ID and sign-in email, and is removed when cleanup is proven.

The app supports one active journal phone. Approving a sign-in on a replacement phone removes the previous phone's server authority before the replacement restores its journal. A previous phone that stays offline cannot be remotely stopped or erased. When it next connects, it cannot update the Drive backup; its encrypted local copy remains until the app is removed or account-deletion cleanup finishes on that phone.

If you already deleted the app, email onelinejournaling+support@gmail.com from the account email. We will erase the One Line account and server data within 30 days. We cannot delete the recovery package inside your Google Drive account.

Current database recovery copies contain account and sign-in records, not journal entries. Copies in provider-held trash, file-version histories, or disaster-recovery systems may persist outside our control. Our normal cleanup keeps the newest account-only recovery copy and may keep an earlier copy for two weeks or longer when delivery cannot be confirmed. Automatic pruning is currently paused during this review, so we cannot promise a two-week deletion date. If new copies stop, the last verified recovery copy may be kept longer until a replacement is safely stored.

After account deletion, one word-free marker may remain for 60 days. It contains the random account ID, deletion time, and a scrambled cleanup verifier so a previously active installation can learn that the account was deleted. It contains no email, name, recovery code, or journal content.

Current provider details: Supabase processes account hosting, authentication, and security logs in the United States. One Line keeps encrypted account-database recovery copies on the operator’s Mac and in the operator’s iCloud Drive and Google Drive accounts. Those copies contain account and sign-in records, including sign-in emails and any stored password hashes, but no journal entries. Brevo processes sign-in email and owner problem alerts and keeps delivery logs for one month. Google Drive stores the recovery package under the Google account you choose. For account-support cases we use Gmail for the thread and a private case index on the operator’s Mac. Some cases also use a private Google Drive case file and Google Calendar for reminders. The local index stores case references, topics, statuses and dates without copying journal writing or email bodies. On or before the case’s delete-by date, no later than 90 days after resolution, we permanently delete each thread, case file, reminder and local case record from our accounts and Mac. These providers may still retain limited security, legal, disaster-recovery, or anonymized information under their own policies.

Children

One Line Journaling is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
