One Line Journaling — Support

Plain help for your account, backup, and recovery.

Contact

Email onelinejournaling+support@gmail.com. For account requests, write from the email address used to sign in so we can confirm the request.

Sign-in codes

Codes can take a minute and sometimes land in spam or junk. You can request a fresh code after the countdown finishes.

Journal recovery

On your current phone, open Settings → Google Drive backup and connect the Google account you want to use. If you lose this phone before its latest writing reaches Google Drive, you may lose that writing. Without Google Drive recovery, you may lose the whole journal.

On a replacement phone, sign in and choose Continue with Google Drive before your first sentence. Use the same Google account. One Line verifies and restores the hidden recovery package without asking for a separate code.

The package contains an encrypted archive and its recovery material. One Line’s servers and Supabase do not receive either value, but someone who controls the chosen Google account and its hidden app data could recover the journal. The original One Line sign-in account is not required for recovery into an empty journal. Provider-held histories may persist, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Delete your account and server data

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account & data. If you no longer have the app, email us from the account email. We will confirm the request and erase the One Line account and server data within 30 days.

Deleting the One Line account does not delete the recovery package owned by your Google account. To remove it too, open Google Drive settings, choose Manage apps, find One Line Journaling, and delete its hidden app data.
