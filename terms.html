Effective 28 September 2026

Using One Line

These terms apply when you use One Line Journaling. If you do not agree to them, do not use the app.

One Line Journaling is a personal journaling tool. It is not medical care, therapy, or professional advice, and it should not be used in place of them.

Your journal belongs to you

You keep ownership of everything you write. One Line stores the journal on your active phone and, if you connect Google Drive from Settings, as a protected recovery package in your hidden Google Drive app data. You give the app only the permission needed to create, read, replace, and restore that package as described in the Privacy Policy.

Accounts and access

Your One Line account, Google account, and phone are personal to you. Keep them secure, and do not use another person’s account or try to access data that is not yours.

Approving a replacement sign-in gives that phone the one server-authorized session. One Line does not sync or merge journals across phones.

Your backup and Google account

Google Drive recovery is optional. If you lose this phone before its latest writing reaches Google Drive, you may lose that writing. Without Google Drive recovery, you may lose the whole journal.

You are responsible for keeping access to the Google account you choose. Its hidden One Line recovery package contains both the encrypted journal archive and the recovery material needed to open it. One Line and Supabase do not receive or keep that package.

If you lose the active phone and cannot access either a connected Google Drive recovery package or a separately saved encrypted backup, your journal may be unrecoverable.

We removed the known older journal-bearing backup files we controlled on 28 September 2026. Provider-held histories may persist, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Cost and availability

One Line Journaling is free. There is no subscription and there are no in-app purchases.

We work to keep One Line Journaling available and your journal protected, but no online service can promise uninterrupted operation. The app is provided as available, to the extent the law allows.

Ending your account

You can delete your One Line account and its server data from Settings at any time. You can also request deletion by emailing us; emailed requests are completed within 30 days.

Account deletion removes the local journal from the active phone but leaves the recovery package in your Google Drive. You must delete that hidden app data through Google Drive settings if you also want it erased.

We may suspend or close an account when reasonably necessary to protect people or the service, respond to unlawful or abusive use, comply with legal duties, or discontinue One Line Journaling. We will give notice when practical, but urgent security or legal action may happen without advance notice.
